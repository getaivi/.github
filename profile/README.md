Aivi is the simplest way to connect your devices to real-time **Live
Activities** and **widgets** on your iPhone. Make a single HTTP call to the
Aivi API and track the progress of anything &ndash; from a washing machine
cycle to a 3D print &ndash; or keep any metric at a glance on your Home or
Lock Screen.

- **Broad support.** iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, CarPlay, and macOS.
- **Shared activities.** Invite people once and a single API call keeps
  everyone in sync.
- **Multiple templates.** Choose between progress bars, multi-value monitors,
  and more &ndash; each with custom colors and icons.
- **Widgets.** Home and Lock Screen widgets for the things you monitor
  &ndash; gauges, trends, battery levels, energy prices, and more. The
  Showcase widget pairs a picture with a state and up to six readings.
- **Buttons.** Switchboard and Remote widgets put buttons and toggles on
  your Home Screen, and the Remote Live Activity puts them on the Lock Screen
  and in the Dynamic Island.
- **Any device.** Add live activities to smart home devices that don't
  natively support them.
- **Power-efficient.** Push-based &ndash; no polling, no wasted battery.

## Get started

1. Download [Aivi from the App Store](https://getaivi.app).
2. Read the [documentation](https://docs.getaivi.app) to set up your first
   live activity.
3. Using Home Assistant? Install the
   [integration](https://docs.getaivi.app/home-assistant/integration/) and
   choose your entities in a panel, with no YAML to write. Prefer plain
   automations? Use the
   [blueprints](https://docs.getaivi.app/home-assistant/blueprints/).
